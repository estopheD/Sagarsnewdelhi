## About

Advocate Rajeev Sagar is the founding and managing partner of Sagar & Sagar Law Offices in New Delhi, a firm he co-founded in 2000. With over two decades of practice, he leads the firm's litigation and dispute resolution work across India. Mr. Sagar regularly appears in the Supreme Court of India, the Delhi High Court, and various tribunals, handling complex matters in [banking, finance](/practice-areas/banking-finance-debt-recovery/), [insolvency](/practice-areas/insolvency-bankruptcy-ibc/), [corporate](/practice-areas/corporate-ma-private-equity-capital-markets/) and [civil law](/practice-areas/litigation-dispute-resolution/). He is known for building a strong record of evidence early in each case and for a pragmatic, results-driven approach. He advises major banks, financial institutions, non-banking financial companies and corporate clients on debt recovery and insolvency issues. In addition to courtroom advocacy, Mr. Sagar participates in legal education and thought leadership (e.g. webinars on debt recovery) and maintains a commitment to rigorous, timely preparation. His leadership style emphasizes commercial awareness and precision — ensuring clients' matters are handled with strategic foresight and meticulous documentation.

## Career chronology

Mr. Sagar began his legal career around 2000, the same year he co-founded Sagar & Sagar Law Offices in New Delhi. Since then he has led the firm's growth into a nationally recognized practice with offices in Delhi NCR and Mumbai, representing clients across India. Under his leadership, the firm has expanded its bench of advocates and broadened its services in banking, finance, corporate and regulatory law. In November 2024, his co-founding partner (and brother) [Sanjeev Sagar](/about/sanjeev-sagar/) was designated a Senior Advocate by the Delhi High Court, reflecting the firm's senior status and Mr. Sagar's own role in building that reputation. As of 2026, Advocate Sagar continues to head the firm's advocacy in high-value litigation and advisory mandates, while also overseeing the [legal process outsourcing (LPO)](/lpo/) services for international clients.

His practice has developed across that entire field rather than within a segment of it, and the firm's structure follows from it: banking, recovery, insolvency and [financial crime defence](/practice-areas/white-collar-financial-crime-defence/) are conducted together, so that a client's position remains coherent as a matter moves between forums.

He is the partner principally responsible for the direction of the firm's practice and for the conduct of its most significant mandates, and he leads its institutional relationships. He also oversees the firm's international legal support practice.

In the early years of his practice, he even handled a diverse range of serious criminal matters, including proceedings involving homicide, murder, theft, arson, extortion and offences under special penal statutes, including the UAPA, while also appearing in proceedings under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA). Over the years, his practice has evolved substantially towards corporate and commercial law, banking and financial services, insolvency, regulatory and high-stakes dispute resolution, with appearances before the Supreme Court, High Courts and specialised tribunals. Today, he focuses primarily on complex corporate-commercial and financial matters, while continuing to draw upon the breadth of his litigation experience to provide commercially grounded legal strategy. His practice is also increasingly extending beyond domestic mandates through [legal process outsourcing (LPO)](/lpo/), cross-border legal support and international legal services, reflecting the Firm's broader focus on technology-enabled and globally integrated legal practice.

In these areas, Mr. Sagar has guided high-value, complex matters. For example, he has represented large banks and NBFCs as counsel in major insolvency and enforcement cases. He has also taken on corporate defendants in commercial disputes. *(Details of specific cases are confidential client matters, but they involve multi-million rupee claims in insolvency and debt recovery.)* Signature matters include leading recovery suits and insolvency petitions on behalf of public sector banks and private financial companies, where he often co-counsels with or appears alongside senior advocates.

His scope of work typically in such matters involves facilitating negotiations, strategic and legal advice and assistance, drafting detailed pleadings, conducting evidence hearings, and presenting oral arguments in appellate courts.

## Professional philosophy and approach

Advocate Sagar is known for a **rigorous, evidence-based approach**. He emphasizes building a strong factual and documentary record from the earliest stage of a case. This means carefully preparing all security documents, notices and pleadings on the assumption that each point will be scrutinized on appeal or enforcement. His professional philosophy combines *commercial awareness* with *procedural precision* — focusing on clients' business realities while navigating complex law and procedure. In client interactions and courtroom advocacy, he prioritizes clarity and results: arguments are tailored to practical outcomes, and communications (written and oral) are kept clear and solution-oriented. He also values **continual learning**: staying updated on new regulations (e.g. [IBC amendments](/insights/insolvency-bankruptcy-code-amendment-act-2026-practitioner-reference/)) and mentoring his team. *(For example, he has participated in legal webinars and training programs on debt recovery and insolvency, sharing insights with junior lawyers.)*

Within the firm, Mr. Sagar instills a culture of teamwork and integrity. He works closely with [Senior Advocate Sanjeev Sagar](/about/sanjeev-sagar/) on appellate and complex matters, ensuring clients have top-level representation. His approach is collaborative internally (leveraging the firm's multi-city offices and LPO resources) and client-focused externally. According to the firm's published philosophy, "we build the record at the earliest stage" — an outlook Mr. Sagar embodies in every case strategy.
